Robotic arms are extremely versatile, which is the entire point. But that versatility comes at the cost of complexity when it comes to programming and control. A few years ago, Kelton Serra from the Build Some Stuff YouTube channel simplified the situation by giving his custom robotic arm a puppet-style controller.
